# Independent research versus the Nivesh workflow

Reviewed 13 October 2026. Companies: Gem Aromatics (`GEMAROMA`), Sambhv Steel Tubes (`SAMBHV`) and Hy-Tech Engineers (`HTEL`). This is a research and product-validation exercise, not a stock recommendation. Prices and provider availability can change. No live Gemini generation was available.

## How I researched them independently

1. Confirmed legal names and exchange symbols against issuer/exchange filings, rather than guessing from similar names.
2. Read the annual income statement and latest quarterly presentation, identifying the reporting basis and original unit before comparing amounts. Rendered and visually inspected the consolidated income pages for all three companies.
3. Read business and concentration disclosures separately from financial tables. Kept historical customers, named suppliers, anonymous counterparties, related parties and proposed arrangements distinct.
4. Compared management's operating explanation with the reported numbers. Asked what would support the explanation, what contradicts it, and which future milestone would test it.
5. Looked for a current earnings transcript, funding/capex evidence and external exposures. A company statement about a policy or weather event remains that company's account until independently corroborated.
6. Evaluated what the app retrieved and indexed, not merely whether a PDF downloaded. The evaluation fixtures are held in [benchmarks/issuer_cases.json](benchmarks/issuer_cases.json); runtime collection never imports them.

## Gem Aromatics

**Financial basis matters materially.** FY26 standalone revenue and profit are ₹370.941 Cr and ₹26.709 Cr; consolidated figures are ₹366.473 Cr and ₹1.425 Cr. The saved provider revenue was ₹363.083 Cr and profit ₹1.425 Cr, with no supplied reporting-basis classification. Its revenue therefore needs definition/basis reconciliation, rather than silently being declared equivalent to the filing. Sources: annual report, physical PDF pages 88 and 138. [Issuer annual report](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/annual_reports/AR_32070_GEMAROMA_2025_2026_U_7002437_22092026175129.pdf).

**The important research question is commercial ramp-up, not installed capacity alone.** Q1FY27 consolidated revenue was approximately ₹98.9 Cr and PAT a ₹7.9 Cr loss. Standalone PAT was approximately ₹7.3 Cr. Management attributes pressure to mix, feedstock costs and the newly capitalised Dahej plant. Depreciation alone cannot explain weaker EBITDA; product margins and utilisation also need testing. Planned qualification/revenue milestones are guidance, not established future sales. The Madagascar flood explanation is management-reported context requiring independent corroboration. [Latest investor presentation](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/GEMAROMATICS2024_13082026191948_Intimation.pdf), physical pages 7–10.

**Concentration needs explicit names and dates.** The prospectus discloses selected customers including Colgate Palmolive (India) Limited, dōTERRA Global Limited and Ventos Do Brasil LTDA. Its FY25 top-ten customer share was 56.06%; several other names were withheld. Do not guess them or treat historical disclosure as proof of current contracts. [Prospectus archive](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/content/ipo/RHP_GEMAROMA.zip), physical pages 33–34.

**What would change my assessment:** recurring qualified-product sales, improving consolidated operating margins and cash conversion would support the ramp-up explanation. Repeated commissioning delays, weak repeat demand or accumulating inventory would weaken it. A valuation conclusion needs a scenario for these drivers, financing and dilution; standalone P/E alone is insufficient.

## Sambhv Steel Tubes

FY26 consolidated revenue and profit are ₹2,413.243 Cr and ₹142.151 Cr. Standalone profit is ₹143.268 Cr. The saved provider revenue was ₹2,411.216 Cr. The small revenue difference is retained for review; similar values do not establish identical definitions. [Annual report](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/annual_reports/AR_30420_SAMBHV_2025_2026_A_15730184_18082026215120.pdf), physical pages 107 and 179.

**Separate production from externally sold output.** The Q1 presentation reports 334,439 tonnes of production, including captive consumption, and 107,771 tonnes of sales. These are different measures. It also reports operating EBITDA per tonne and capacity/consent milestones. Adding intermediate and finished production would overstate saleable scale; consent to establish, consent to operate and actual commissioning must remain distinct. The presentation's operating figures are explicitly standalone. [Q1FY27 presentation](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/SAMBHAV2024_03082026212932_Investor_Presentation.pdf), physical pages 7–8 and 15.

The company's organisation page explicitly names NMDC and SECL in its raw-material supplier discussion. This supports source-labelled supplier mentions; it does not quantify purchases or establish current contract terms. Anonymous PSU descriptions elsewhere must not be mapped to these names by inference. [Company organisation page](https://www.sambhv.com/our-organization.php).

**What I would test:** finished-product volumes, realised prices, operating EBITDA per tonne, incremental working capital and expansion funding. Capacity growth can coincide with weaker spreads or excess industry supply. Any PLI/import-protection argument needs the exact eligible products, effective rules and evidence of receipt, not a general policy headline. The transcript is a source for management's expectations and challenges, not independent proof of future results.

## Hy-Tech Engineers

The FY26 consolidated presentation reports revenue of approximately ₹189.4 Cr and PAT ₹22.6 Cr. The saved provider baseline was ₹182.673 Cr and ₹22.592 Cr. Revenue differs materially while profit is close; combining them without a basis check can distort the margin. Original table values are in millions, so conversion to crore divides by ten. [Latest presentation](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/HYTECH_21092026092158_Q1_Press_Release_Investor_Presentation.pdf), physical page 22.

**Customer tables span pages.** The prospectus names Tompkins Industries Inc, Sisa Hydropneumatics, Hy-Tech USA Inc, Fittings Unlimited Inc and DAN Hydraulics, while withholding other names. FY26 top-ten customers account for 45.32% of reported revenue. These are disclosed relationships; group ownership, independence and current status require separate checks. [Prospectus archive](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/content/ipo/RHP_HTEL.zip), physical pages 23–24; [SEBI final-prospectus listing](https://www.sebi.gov.in/filings/public-issues/aug-2026/hy-tech-engineers-limited-prospectus_104093.html).

**The driver is equipment demand and repricing.** Q1 commentary links margin pressure to raw-material costs and delays in customer price revisions. Fitting-piece capacity and forging tonnage are different physical measures. [Latest presentation](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/HYTECH_21092026092158_Q1_Press_Release_Investor_Presentation.pdf), physical pages 2–3 and 14–15.

**The app corrected my initial research's recency gap.** September's presentation described overseas entities as conditional plans. A later filing reports HY-TECH ENGINEERS Inc incorporated in Delaware on October 5, with the certificate received on October 7. Incorporation is now established; operating activity and revenue are not. The similarly named historical customer Hy-Tech USA Inc must not be merged with this entity. [October 7 incorporation filing](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/HYTECH_07102026201042_Incorporation_intimation.pdf).

**What would change my assessment:** evidence of price recovery, repeat OEM/distributor orders and profitable export growth would support the thesis. Demand weakness, customer losses, expansion delays or increasing working-capital needs would challenge it. A US customer alone does not establish net US tariff or currency exposure.

## What the baseline program missed and what changed

| Baseline problem | Implemented response |
| --- | --- |
| Three downloaded documents looked like sufficient research; all three graphs were empty | More source-type diversity, transcripts, explicit named-list/table extraction and issuer business HTML. Missing names remain unknown. |
| A newspaper clipping introduced unrelated loan notices/articles into HTEL evidence | News clippings and routine notices are excluded from automatic issuer analysis and fallback selection. |
| Extensionless `/doc/…` investor downloads were missed; old undated documents could outrank newer ones | Semantic download-link detection and labelled period sorting hints, separate from publication dates. |
| Customer tables continued onto another page | A narrowly scoped continuation rule retains the preceding verified table role. |
| Splitting every “and” fragmented real legal names | Legal suffixes control list separation; internal conjunctions and Unicode aliases are retained. |
| Provider amounts had no independent filing comparison | Conservative explicit income-table parsing, original units/basis/period/page, visible differences and a same-table annual baseline selector. Values are not silently overwritten. |
| Generic keywords prioritised boilerplate and missed operating economics | Operating, guidance and concentration categories, boilerplate penalties and business-specific diligence templates with reviewable evidence links. |

The templates are questions inferred from business descriptions, not stored answers for these issuers. Matched evidence is marked for review, not as an answered question. The benchmark measures coverage of selected independently checked facts; it is not a general financial-accuracy score.

## Remaining boundaries

OCR, arbitrary PDF table layouts, automatic consolidation/related-party judgment, exhaustive supplier/customer registries, comprehensive global-event discovery and semantic verification of AI reasoning remain incomplete. Ambiguous table layouts are skipped. Generic macro articles do not establish issuer impact. The beginner scenario still holds current shares fixed and does not model debt, tax or dilution separately.
