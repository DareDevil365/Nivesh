# Route questions to primary evidence

Choose sources after identifying the business exposure and research question. Do not treat this list as live data or a complete source catalog. Verify current publication dates, definitions, reporting periods and access availability when using a source. A blocked page is a collection gap; do not bypass access controls or infer its contents.

| Question | Starting source | Evidence to retain |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What has the issuer disclosed? | Company investor relations; [NSE announcements](https://www.nseindia.com/companies-listing/corporate-filings-announcements) and [financial results](https://www.nseindia.com/companies-listing/corporate-filings-financial-results); BSE filings when applicable | Original attachment, legal entity, publication date, financial period, consolidated/standalone basis and page |
| How do competitors make money? | Each competitor's original annual report and operating disclosures | Comparable product/geography definitions, volumes, pricing, capacity, margins and reporting basis; do not infer a competitive relationship from industry labels alone |
| How do rates, credit and currency conditions affect the company? | [RBI press releases](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_PressReleaseDisplay.aspx), official policy statements and [DBIE](https://dbie.rbi.org.in/) | Exact policy date, rate/series definition, loan repricing, customer financing, net currency exposure and hedging evidence |
| Is demand changing with the economy? | [MoSPI](https://www.mospi.gov.in/), RBI surveys and official industry data | GDP/CPI/IIP or relevant survey definition, release versus reference date, revisions and seasonality; connect the series to the company's actual end market |
| Does government spending or policy create an opportunity? | [Union Budget](https://www.indiabudget.gov.in/), [PIB](https://www.pib.gov.in/), responsible ministry/regulator, original rule and award records | Proposed/notified status, eligibility, effective dates, budget allocation versus expenditure, order awards, execution and cash collection |
| How might global conditions affect the thesis? | [IMF World Economic Outlook](https://www.imf.org/en/Publications/WEO), [IEA outlooks](https://www.iea.org/topics/world-energy-outlook), and relevant national/statistical agencies | Scenario assumptions and publication dates, company/customer geographic exposure and transmission mechanism; forecasts are not observed facts |

Use reputable news and secondary industry research to find developments and contradictory views, then trace material factual claims to original releases where possible. Label commissioned industry estimates, management guidance and sell-side forecasts by origin and incentives. Do not copy a brokerage recommendation as an independent conclusion.

For a live research question, search the resolved company/sector plus the specific driver and the relevant authority. Read the original item, the issuer's exposure note and at least one meaningful counterargument. Record the expected operational effect, financial bridge, timing and falsification metric. State explicitly when the required source is inaccessible or not disclosed.

Runtime scope: NSE/company document discovery, bounded reading, PIB/RBI feed leads and allowlisted official HTML context are implemented. Headlines and articles remain unverified impact leads, with source failures visible. Broad web/news search, automatic macro time-series joins, policy implementation tracking and comprehensive peer discovery are not connected. A Codex-assisted investigation can use this source map with browsing tools; that is separate from the background collector. The [PIB RSS directory](https://www.pib.gov.in/ViewRss.aspx?lang=1&reg=1) documents its public feeds; successful regional/English access must still be checked at runtime.
